About ClearCue

A clearer path through an eye-drop routine.

Why ClearCue exists

Eye-drop routines can be difficult to keep up with when there are multiple medicines, different eyes, or several daily times.

ClearCue is an iPhone-first personal routine companion. It helps people keep medication reminders, daily progress, and a simple patient summary together on their phone.

It is built around a straightforward principle: people should be able to follow their clinician’s plan without surrendering control of their routine information.

Our principle

ClearCue supports your clinician’s plan.

Focused

One focused place for reliable eye-drop routines.

Accessible

Large text, high contrast, reduced motion, color-safe labels, and Spanish support.

Patient-controlled

Information stays on the device and reports are shared only when the person chooses.

Medical-information disclaimer

ClearCue does not diagnose, prescribe, validate a clinical treatment plan, or replace your clinician’s instructions or prescription label. For urgent symptoms, follow your clinician’s emergency instructions or local emergency guidance.
